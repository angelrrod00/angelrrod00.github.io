## Background and inspiration

I started producing around 2015 in Ableton, after taking a keen interest in expressing myself through music. A close friend — a certified genius — could riff his emotions on the piano effortlessly, and I was fascinated by it. Then I stumbled onto deadmau5's streams, where he was producing live, and I was hooked.

By 2017 I had settled into my dorm at the University of Michigan, but I was missing a space where my expression felt at home. Turns out engineering campus has a few studios. I took the effort to get all the certifications required to use the rooms, and started renting hours.

## Process

I never found my "workflow," but I was obsessed with the pursuit of one — a black hole of never feeling qualified enough for authentic exploration.

Then one day, after a long one, I stopped trying to do it, and in frustration just began to riff.

I liked using sequencers. I liked my synth. I had small chains of workflow that made nice sounds reliably. So — why not plug this here, and this into that, and how about I just fuckin—

And bam.

Out of a chaotic mess layered from chaotic pieces, I found a emergent and pleasant sounding order - and it was representative of exactly what I had just discovered... completely self-referentially.

## Result

An intro to a song. Disorganized noises, each with its own inspired idea, rushing together toward the angst, arriving at an order discovered by simply moving forward.

I titled the file on my machine `my greatest accomplishment`.

## Where my head's at now

I think it represents structured beauty emerging — or showing itself — out of the entropy.

The universe has a law to resolve itself, at least it seems that way. It is a gift to be part of its dance, and it may just be physically impossible not to be.
