## Background and inspiration

Light Commands is the discovery that you can inject voice commands into a smart speaker or phone by pointing a modulated laser at its microphone — no sound, no proximity, no code on the device. The MEMS microphone responds to light as though it were sound.

I dare not take anything away from the massive efforts of Dr. Benjamin Cyr and Dr. Sara Rampazzi on this project. I was honored to be a fly on the wall throughout the process.

## Process

My contribution here was presence and a camera. All of the photographs on the project's website were taken by me.

What I witnessed was science and beautiful raw human effort, ingenuity, and dedication.
